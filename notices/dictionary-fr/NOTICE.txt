Source availability

index.aff and index.dic are the dictionary's source files, shipped verbatim:
inside app.asar at node_modules/dictionary-fr/ on desktop, with this notice in
the resources directory's licenses/dictionary-fr/, and in dict/<language>/ in
NEO Pocket, beside this notice.
