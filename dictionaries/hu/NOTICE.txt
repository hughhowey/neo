Source availability

index.aff and index.dic are LibreOffice's hu_HU/hu_HU.aff and
hu_HU/hu_HU.dic, unchanged but for their names. They ship with every
packaged app, beside this notice, in
  app.asar.unpacked/node_modules/dictionary-hu/

Upstream: https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/master/hu_HU
(as of 2026-10-06)
Dictionary project: http://magyarispell.sf.net

NEO's own spellcheck (WebAssembly Hunspell) never asked for these files
in another form: Hunspell reads them as distributed.
