Ukrainian spelling dictionary

NEO distributes the dictionary and affix data of the Ukrainian
spelling dictionary 6.7.5, built from the dict_uk project, as published
by the LibreOffice project under the Mozilla Public License 1.1.
NEO's MIT license does not replace the dictionary's.

oxt package authors: Andriy Rysin, 2007-2017.
Dictionary project: https://github.com/brown-uk/dict_uk
The upstream notice is README_uk_UA.txt; the license is MPL-1.1.txt.

Source availability

index.aff and index.dic are LibreOffice's uk_UA/uk_UA.aff and
uk_UA/uk_UA.dic, unchanged but for their names. They ship with every
packaged app, beside this notice, in
  app.asar.unpacked/node_modules/dictionary-uk/

Upstream: https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries/tree/master/uk_UA
(commit 589a31b2c7e8a592270012e111a51a80d0176f6e, as of 2026-11-07)

NEO's own spellcheck (WebAssembly Hunspell) never asked for these files
in another form: Hunspell reads them as distributed.
