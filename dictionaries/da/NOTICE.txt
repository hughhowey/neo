Source availability

index.aff and index.dic are LibreOffice's da_DK/da_DK.aff and
da_DK/da_DK.dic, unchanged but for their names. They ship with every
packaged app, beside this notice, in
  app.asar.unpacked/node_modules/dictionary-da/
and in NEO Pocket in dict/da/.
