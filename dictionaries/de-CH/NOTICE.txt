Swiss Standard German spelling dictionary

NEO distributes the Swiss German (de_CH) "frami" dictionary and affix
data, as published by the LibreOffice project, under its GNU General
Public License option (version 2 or version 3). The OASIS 0.1
alternative is not removed. NEO's MIT license does not replace the
dictionary's.

Based on the igerman98 dictionary by Björn Jacke
(https://www.j3e.de/ispell/igerman98/), with the "frami" additions by
Franz Michael Baumann. Swiss spelling: ss, never ß.
The upstream notice is README_de_DE_frami.txt (ISO-8859-1, as published);
the license texts are COPYING_GPLv2, COPYING_GPLv3 and COPYING_OASIS.txt.

Source availability

index.aff and index.dic are LibreOffice's de/de_CH_frami.aff and
de/de_CH_frami.dic, converted from ISO-8859-1 to UTF-8 (iconv), with the
affix file's "SET ISO8859-1" line changed to "SET UTF-8" to match.
Nothing else is changed. NEO's WebAssembly Hunspell matches words as
UTF-8, so the ISO-8859-1 originals reject every word with an umlaut.
They ship with every packaged app, beside this notice, in
  app.asar.unpacked/node_modules/dictionary-de-ch/
and in NEO Pocket in dict/de-CH/.
