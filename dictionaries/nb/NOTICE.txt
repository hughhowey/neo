Norwegian Bokmål spelling dictionary

NEO distributes the Norwegian Bokmål (nb_NO) dictionary and affix data of
LibreOffice's Norwegian dictionaries, version 3.0 (maintainer Lars
Bungum), as published by the LibreOffice project. NEO's MIT license does
not replace the dictionary's terms, which are:

- the word list (index.dic): Ordbanken, Nasjonalbiblioteket, under
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
  with new headwords from the 2018-2024 revision of Bokmålsordboka,
  CLARINO Bergen / Språkbanken (http://hdl.handle.net/11509/152), under
  CLARIN PUB +BY, and Språkrådet's words of the year;
- the affix file (index.aff): the spell-norwegian project, under the GNU
  General Public License version 2 (COPYING).

The upstream notice, with every source and its attribution, is
README_NO.txt.

Source availability

index.aff and index.dic are LibreOffice's no/nb_NO.aff and
no/nb_NO.dic, unchanged but for their names. They ship with every
packaged app, beside this notice, in
  app.asar.unpacked/node_modules/dictionary-nb/
and in NEO Pocket in dict/nb/.
