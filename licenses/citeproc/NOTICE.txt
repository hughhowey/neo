citeproc-js citation processor

NEO sets a paper's citations and reference list with citeproc-js 2.4.63
(the npm package citeproc, by Frank Bennett), the processor inside Zotero
and Mendeley. It ships unmodified in app.asar under node_modules/citeproc,
and NEO Pocket carries the same file as paper/vendor/citeproc.js.
