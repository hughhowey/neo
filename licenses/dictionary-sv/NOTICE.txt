Source availability

index.aff and index.dic are the dictionary's source files, shipped verbatim
with this notice in app.asar.unpacked/node_modules/dictionary-sv/ on desktop
and dict/sv/ in Pocket. On macOS the desktop directory is inside
NEO.app/Contents/Resources; on Windows and Linux it is inside resources.
An AppImage can be extracted with --appimage-extract. These files remain
separate from NEO's code and may be replaced with a modified dictionary.

Exact source package:
https://registry.npmjs.org/dictionary-sv/-/dictionary-sv-4.0.0.tgz
