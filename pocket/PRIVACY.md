# NEO Pocket — Privacy Policy

NEO Pocket does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Your writing is stored as plain files on your device. If you have iCloud
Drive turned on, those files also live in your own iCloud account, under
iCloud Drive › NEO Pocket, so that NEO on your Mac can open the same books.
Apple carries those files between your devices; NEO Pocket never sees them
leave your device and has no server of its own.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no accounts, and no
third-party services. It makes no network requests.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/hughhowey/neo/issues.
